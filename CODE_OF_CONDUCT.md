# Code of conduct

Fireweed Queue project spaces are for constructive, technical collaboration.
This policy applies to issues, project discussions, and other interactions
managed by the project maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas and behavior, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or sensitive information.
- Give others room to correct mistakes and disengage from unproductive debate.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, spam, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a concern that can safely be described in public, open an issue and clearly
mark it as a conduct report. Do not put private or sensitive information in that
issue. For sensitive concerns tied to GitHub content, use GitHub's built-in
**Report abuse or spam** action on the relevant account or content. The project
does not currently publish a separate private conduct-reporting inbox.

Maintainers may edit or hide content, lock or close threads, remove participants
from project spaces, or restrict future participation. They will consider the
context, severity, and pattern of behavior when deciding enforcement. A report
does not guarantee a particular action or response time.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this reporting
process.
