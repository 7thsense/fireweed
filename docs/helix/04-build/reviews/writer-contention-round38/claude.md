## Summary

The freshness half of round 37 is genuinely fixed: commit-then-`BEGIN` on the same pooled connection, corroborated by an independent non-writer connection, is the right witness for the property the exact-coverage protocol and the 16+8 reused connections rest on, and it holds under either pinning rule, so the step-7 restatement is sound. The representativeness half is not. As written, the forced-spill clause cannot be satisfied by a production-configured writer, does not say when WAL growth is sampled — and with autocheckpoint disabled a post-commit sample passes while proving nothing — names no instrument that does not itself checkpoint, and treats the strongest possible outcome ("Turso never publishes uncommitted frames") as a program stop. Separately, keeping the serving-reader correction in S-0 is defensible, but the 128 MiB → 4 MiB cut is not scoped to the four validation sites it is described against: it lands on `render_claimed` and the `server_*` observation reads through `TursoRelational::query`, i.e. the measured bottleneck path, immediately before the SHA that becomes the only rate baseline, with no before/after evidence and no named test covering those consumers. Fix the spill clause's satisfiability, sample point, instrument, and no-growth disposition; scope and measure the serving-reader change or defer its cache reduction to a slice S0 can bracket. The remaining nine notes fit inside the existing named acceptance criteria.
