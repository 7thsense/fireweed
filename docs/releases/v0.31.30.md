# Fireweed v0.31.30 — s3 × Turso capacity path

This source-preview prerelease is the public s3 object-log × Turso cell with
honest local MinIO, labeled capacity reports, and a disposable Turso
projection that rebuilds from the log. It is not a governed product-ready
P20pr deployment qualification. An untraced eight-cycle campaign completed
on this source and did **not** meet the 12.5k stretch floor.

## Changes

- Workload and qualification reports label the cell `s3--turso`. Local tests
  still open `StorageConfig::s3_turso` against MinIO; they no longer pretend
  the log is a filesystem object-log.
- Local MinIO object-log data stays on disk (`FIREWEED_MINIO_DATA`, or
  `/var/tmp` when `/tmp` is tmpfs). Capacity runs no longer fill a ramdisk.
- Shards share one S3 client per endpoint and retry dispatch/service/reset
  errors inside the existing 30s produce budget. Concurrent S3 produces are
  capped at 16 so that timer is not spent waiting in a 64-way stampede.
- Log-backed Turso writers set `wal_autocheckpoint=0`. Passive checkpoints
  are admitted at most two process-wide when the WAL is large, and on idle
  compact. Crash recovery remains the object-log, not the Turso file.
- Empty-projection rebuild applies 256-command pages (per-command fallback).
  A crash-loss test rebuilds 20k items from the log alone in about 1s in
  release (~20k items/s) and checks request-id replay plus live-item
  identity.

## Capacity (not a qualification pin)

Untraced eight-cycle campaign on `bcef51f9` (ancestor of this source), disk
MinIO, `--stretch`, 1M items × 8, 64 shards: **9,507 completed lifecycles/s**,
842s wall, 8 cycles finished. Stretch 12.5k failed. Cycles 3 and 6 still dip
when the 400 MiB WAL safety valve checkpoints two stores at a time. This
release does not claim the 10k or 12.5k floors.

