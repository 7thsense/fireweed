## Headline

Packed Claim-then-Complete apply updates a contiguous rowid slice instead of
scanning remaining Pending rows. The projection keeps one Pending FIFO index
for Claim and peek; overlapping group-due and active-scope indexes are dropped.

## Cut contents

- Complete a fused Claim wave with `UPDATE ... rowid BETWEEN` after two
  endpoint primary-key lookups when the named ids occupy consecutive rowids.
- Drop `fireweed_items_group_due_idx` and `fireweed_items_active_scope_idx` on
  migrate; keep `fireweed_items_pending_order_idx` for Claim/peek order.
- Join Claim-then-Complete envelopes in one apply generation; skip lease-bearer
  inserts on that Pending-to-Complete path.

Every Seventh Sense phase on `filesystem--turso` N=10k inflight=8 meets 10k
settled items/s with T3 exact residuals. T1 and T2 N=100k gates remain unmet.
