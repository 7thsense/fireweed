## Headline

Ordinary item mutations stay on one packed add/update path. Claim realize is
take-next-batch: one ordered serving-reader SELECT of due rows with bodies.
A full sequencer generation force-seals instead of paying a second object-log
linger. Apply composes Claim-then-Complete of the same ids into one
Pending-to-Complete update. ClientItemKey BatchUpdate plans without a
serving-reader row snapshot.

## Cut contents

- Select due Claim rows with payloads in schedule order; skip overlay ids
  in-process; do not lease before append.
- Force-seal packed appends that already fill eight envelopes or 800 items.
- Coalesce a following Complete generation of the same nonzero item ids in
  one apply vector; keep the eight-envelope cap for unrelated work.
- Maintain grouped representatives from lease RETURNING plus one correlated
  LIMIT 1 seek per lost head.
- Plan ClientItemKey BatchUpdate without expected versions against an empty
  snapshot (pipelined packed Update).

This cut does **not** claim that every Seventh Sense phase sustains 10k settled
items/s. P4 deliver ack crossed that floor; settled deliver remains apply-bound.
T1 and T2 N=100k gates remain unmet.
