## Headline

Turso applies compatible grouped API-001 schedule replacements through a
bind-bounded, operation-shaped path instead of broadly materializing current
rows and rewriting schedule-sensitive indexes item by item. Exact pre-update
eligibility partitioning and set-based group-summary maintenance preserve queue
semantics while removing the measured P3 rewrite cliff.

## Cut contents

- Upgrade the executable Turso boundary to 0.7.2 and attribute writer wait,
  cursor/definition reads, row reads, update/side-table work, and commit time.
- Specialize full-replacement enrichment and schedule batches while retaining
  exact conditional, leased-item, terminal-item, replay, and request-order
  fallbacks.
- Partition due and future rows using pre-update eligibility so due-to-future,
  future-to-due, and future-to-future transitions update group summaries
  exactly.
- Maintain grouped representatives with set-based updates and a migrated,
  reopen-safe `rep_created_seq` ordering key.
- Preserve bounded lifecycle coordination, append contention identities, and
  committed Turso reader semantics added since v0.31.21.
- Preserve the Snorri P7x quarantine-revalidation attestation and the complete
  phased performance history on `main`.

The focused 10,000-row/800-replacement schedule profile improved from
4,052,016 microseconds before the grouped fast path to about 80,234
microseconds after it. The exact lifecycle P3 rate improved from 306 items/s
to about 10.2k items/s.

This cut does **not** claim that every Seventh Sense phase sustains 10k items/s.
P1 ingest and P4 delivery remain below that target, and the schedule bead stays
open until one quiet-host same-SHA run also satisfies its P2/P4 preservation
floors.
