## Headline

Object-log produce now takes metadata-permit then produce-lock as a global
suffix. File-backed Turso opens inert 16-driver/8-outcome committed reader
pools without switching serving. A continuous Seventh Sense streaming
evidence lane replenishes in-flight batches instead of `join_all` waves.
Force-sealed packs charge exact envelope bytes, transfer follower
reservations to the leader, and distinguish pre-position cancel from
post-position poison. Class-S SQL leases abort when the following append is
cancelled before a position is allocated.

## Cut contents

- Enforce metadata-permit → produce-lock on every object-log produce path,
  with permit-held high-water and append/epoch-acquire/emission-cursor wait
  counters.
- Construct inert committed reader pools (4 MiB, `query_only=1`, busy
  timeout 100 ms) beside the live 128 MiB serving reader.
- Add `ss_streaming` continuous replenishment evidence; the phased smoke
  keeps its phase barriers and retries retryable Backpressure.
- Exact force-seal, typed `PackedAppendError::{BeforePosition,PostPositionAmbiguous}`,
  and leader-owned publication.
- Abort Class-S leases on pre-position cancel so cancelled PUTs have no
  durable serving effect.

This cut does **not** activate committed serving reads, log-first Claim, or
the T1/T2/T3 N=100k gates.
